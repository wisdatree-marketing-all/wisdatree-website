This is a draft notice for the new website. It should be checked against Wisdatree's actual privacy and data handling practices and applicable law before launch.

Information collected

When you submit an enquiry, we request your name, email address, contact number, location (optional), selected topic or programme, and any additional information you voluntarily enter. Business enquirers may provide an organization name; school enquirers may provide a learner's age or grade.

Why we use it

We use the information to respond to enquiries, discuss relevant programmes, and follow up on your stated interests. Do not submit sensitive medical information, identification documents or payment credentials through these forms.

Form processing

Our website uses the third-party your default email app service to deliver form responses to marketing@wisdatree.com. The submitted data is transmitted to and processed by this provider. Review the provider's privacy practices before sending information.

Your choices

To request access, correction or deletion of enquiry information held by Wisdatree, contact marketing@wisdatree.com. Any retention periods and additional statutory rights will be governed by Wisdatree's finalized policy and applicable law.

Website hosting

Website files are served through GitHub Pages when deployed there. GitHub may process site visitor technical information as explained in its privacy documentation.
